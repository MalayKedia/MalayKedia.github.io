About

I am Malay Kedia, a fourth-year B.Tech student in Computer Science & Engineering at IIT Bombay.

I'm currently doing my bachelor's thesis on Reasoning in VLMs with Prof. Preethi Jyothi, surveying failure modes of open source MLLMs on visual reasoning puzzles and investigating techniques to improve their performance using both inference-time and training-time interventions.

Last summer, I worked as an Algorithm Development Intern at Hudson River Trading in Singapore, developing classical ML and deep-learning models on order-book data to design trading strategies, and building a fair-value model for overnight equity returns informed by global market movements and Bloomberg news. Before that, I spent time at Uppsala University, working with Prof. Parosh Abdulla and Prof. Krishna S on rewriting semantics for weak memory models (paper in review).

Outside research and coursework, I love watching movies and web series, and listening to Bollwood music. I also mentor for the Indian Astronomy Olympiad, TA for CSE courses, and volunteer with Krittika, IIT Bombay's astronomy club.

Feel free to reach out if you'd like to chat about anything from research to movies to music, or if you have any questions about my work or experience!
